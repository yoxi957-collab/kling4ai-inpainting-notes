A “Doomsday Warning” for Film and Television Post-Production Companies? Breaking Down Kling 4.0’s Localized Inpainting and 15 Groups of Multimodal Assets: Reshoots and Starting Over Become History!

In addition to generation, put modification and version management back into the production process.

In the traditional audiovisual industry and 3D CG rendering industry, the most expensive and painful cost is never the first production, but the modification.

The client's words, "Change this actor to an overseas face," "Change the sunny day in the background to a rainy night," and "The drink packaging on the table must be replaced with the latest batch," were enough to make a post-production special effects team work overtime for a week, and even had to arrange for re-shooting on site. This is even more true with AI video tools in the past - as long as you want to change a tiny element in the picture, even just one word, the model will randomly refresh the entire face and the entire room, and all previous efforts will be instantly wiped out.

At this critical point, Kuaishou’s Kling 4.0 has introduced a full suite of targeted video-editing tools and an asset matrix beyond previous limits, creating a devastating replacement effect on traditional post-production pipelines.

1. Precise Targeted Video Editing: Change What You See and Leave Complete Restarts Behind

Kling 4.0 is the first to deeply integrate the originally extremely separated "video generation" and "directional video editing" at the industrial level:

  • Free replacement of subjects and scenes: Through a simple combination of text and reference images, creators can directly specify a partial subject in an existing video to be eliminated, replaced or added, while the rest of the picture, light, shadow, tones and camera movements are not affected at all.
  • Seamless reconstruction of the physical environment: You can directly give instructions to a 30-second long video that has been generated: "Replace the background from the beach during the day to the cyberpunk rainy night, and the light and shadow materials are synchronized to cold neon reflections." Kling 4.0 can instantly complete the relighting operation of the entire environment and materials while retaining the original character's movements and emotions.

This means that the extensive processes that once required tracking points, keying against green screens, 3D remodeling and render compositing are now compressed into a text instruction and a texture image.

2. An Asset Pool of 15 Groups Beyond Previous Limits: Absolute Control over Products and Characters

For commercial brands, Kling 4.0 is an insurmountable peak. It allows up to 10 high-definition pictures, 5 videos and 7 core subjects to be mounted at a time:

  • Brands can submit detail images of 3 core products, assets for 2 designated digital humans, 1 camera-movement reference and 1 action reference together in a single package.
  • During computation, the model behaves like a meticulous asset-pipeline scheduler, firmly anchoring each subject to its own semantic layer. Earlier AI videos suffered fatal flaws such as “a brand logo deforming as soon as the head turns” or “a product’s bevel disappearing under side lighting.” Kling 4.0’s powerful all-round reference constraints completely eliminate these defects.

For commercial short dramas and promotional films with more content, Kling 4.0’s planned multi-round seamless continuation of up to 2 minutes completely breaks the duration barrier.
